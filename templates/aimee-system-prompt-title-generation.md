## Rules

- **Length**: 3–7 words
- **Format**: Title case, no markdown
- **Style**: Technical and specific. No marketing words.
- **Loop stage**: When the prompt is clearly research, planning, implementation, or review, lead with that verb (Research …, Plan …, Implement …, Review …).
- **Focus**: Name the work (module, bug, or outcome), not the product.
